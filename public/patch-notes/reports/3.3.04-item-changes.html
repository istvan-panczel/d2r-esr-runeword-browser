Lines marked old → new changed value; − removed; + added. The docs now print every bleed length that read “over %.2f seconds” in 3.3.03 (85 lines on the unique and runeword pages) as the game file length ÷ 25 seconds, the bleed plugin’s rule; the game files did not change, so items with only that difference are not listed. That makes Throatslitter’s 0.12 seconds (the 3.3.03 report read it as 3). Patch-note fixes with no stat-line change: physical damage added as elemental or poison damage now works again on Voidforge, Icing Death, Earthshifter, Gargoyle’s Bite and Doomfletch’s Prism (skills.txt raises the floor of their damage-bonus term from −90 to 0; Gargoyle’s Bite now reads your throwing damage). “Ironbolt life on striking” is the Ironbolt ascendancy, not a unique: see the Ascendancy section. Verdicts are a reading of the stat lines, and nothing here was tested in game.

Ascendancy Stones (removed)

ESR 3.3.04 removes the Ascendancy Stone and moves the whole system into a panel. All 1,859 ascendancy rows of cubemain.txt are gone (stone recipes, tier transmutes, soulstone captures, Lich King bonus and their trap rows). The tier bonuses themselves are the same as the old recipes, with the exceptions listed below; tier 3 and 4 scaling formulas only gained the Lich King factor. Old checks on the never-set stat ascendancy_upgrade were removed from the tier 3 auras; they had no effect.

Kill Ledger

Boss kills used to cast a “Just Killed …” curse on players near the boss for 60 seconds (monprop.txt death skills; aura range 100 in skills.txt). 3.3.04 removes those kill-credit death skills from all 24 boss rows in monprop.txt (including Nihlathak and Uber Tristram, which now drive ascendancy tiers 2 and 3) and the plugin gives the same kill credit value to every living player in the boss's level for 1500 frames (60 s). The “Just Killed …” skills are still in skills.txt, unchanged. The Kill Ledger item, its 91 cube rows, Rathma's Eternal Skull recipes and the docs page are unchanged, so every bonus value is unchanged.

  • Gemwords: No gemword changed (gemwords page and game files identical).

Other item changes

  • Formula changes without a stat-line change: Famine, Dreamflange, Agent of the Light and Barbaric Devastator now scale with the Blacksmith Modifier Magnitude perk. As written in skills.txt these formulas multiply by 10 (11 with the perk) without dividing back, unlike the other items’ formulas, which reads as ten times the old effect (a hundred times for Dreamflange and Agent of the Light’s damage). That looks like a slip in the files; not tested in game.
  • New magic suffix: “of Skill Master”, +(10 to 20) to Pounce, on spears (item level 96, required level 60).
  • Alien Artefacts: now stack in the materials tab (patch note: they could not be deposited in the currency tab).
  • Flask of Renewal and Nirvana Grass: now go to the belt when picked up.
