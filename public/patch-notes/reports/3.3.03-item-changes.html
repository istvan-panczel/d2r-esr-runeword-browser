Other item changes

  • Corruption: Magic items can now be corrupted. Every quality except runewords uses one pool per item slot. A failed corruption now always gives a Rare item of the same type; crafted, low, normal and superior items used to keep their quality.
  • Vessel of Souls: Its anointment is now stored and only applied when you transform the Vessel into a mythical weapon, rolling that weapon type’s normal pool. The separate Vessel anointment list is gone.
  • D-stoning: Caps now land on round numbers: Attack Speed and Cast Rate 121 → 120, Attack Rating and damage to demons or undead 504 → 500, Crushing Blow and Deadly Strike 102 → 100, Light Radius 12 → 10, Magic Damage Reduced 48 → 45.
  • Rune forging: Sa rune now forges the right bonus on gloves. Orbs and the Mythical Arcane Staff can be rune forged.
  • Charms: Rerolls keep the charm type. Small and Grand Charms can hold one socket, but Larzuk refuses every charm. A socketed Deviant Cell can be upgraded with the Standard of Heroes.
  • Power Overwhelming: Gemwords can no longer be made into magic items, and the regular magic item socket recipe is off while you have the perk.
  • Ancient Scrolls: A scroll can be downgraded one number in the cube again.
  • Token of Absolution: New recipes for two and three tokens at once.
  • Map bosses: Pandemonium key from tier 2, 3 and 4 bosses: 25% → 35%, 50% and 75%. Tier 5 map from Rathma and Lucion: 25% → 50%. The terrorized Lich King keeps his Frostmourne / Essence roll.
  • Stash: All Alien Artefacts fit in the currency tab.
  • Skill: Wisdom of Athulua starts with +10% to all speeds.
