Physical damage as extra elemental damage

3.3 rewrote the formula of every item that adds part of your physical damage as extra elemental or poison damage. Per the patch notes, elemental crits no longer double-dip on spell damage, stats are no longer counted twice (these items were “dealing way more damage than intended”), and elemental crits now work at any Deadly Strike value. The new formulas also get a bonus from the Blacksmith specialization perks. Expect less damage than in 3.2.24 from all of them; only Voidforge's displayed stats changed.

Patterns worth knowing

  • Many “x% Deadly Strike” lines became the new “x% Deadly Strike Damage” stat (The Annihilator, Razor's Edge…), and many items gained it.
  • New stats show up on reworked items: Crushing Blow Damage, +x to Maximum Curses, Utility Potion Effectiveness / Duration, Increased Effect of Gems / Runes / Jewels Socketed in this Item.
  • Low-chance “on striking” procs often became high-chance “on melee attack” procs.
  • Some “Level x Aura When Equipped” lines became “+x to the skill” instead (Hacker – Fanaticism, Atlastrust – Might, Icon of Sin – Meditation): you now have to run the aura yourself.
  • Weapon masteries on uniques only changed wording, but the patch reworked two masteries themselves: Reflex Bow (Deadly Precision) and Sorceress Orb, which now triples spell cooldowns (Staff of the Horadrim).
  • Fixes without stat changes: The Ties That Bind can drop; Yaerius' Celestial Beacon no longer uses up ammo (get a new one); Ravenlord / Head of the Cerberus wolf limit; larger unique charms can be upgraded with Standard of Heroes; item effects with a condition now give 0 instead of 1 when the condition is not met.
