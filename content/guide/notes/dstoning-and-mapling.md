### D-stoning

:term[D-stoning] cubes an item with one of the Dragon Stone forms, sometimes with a Key or potion as a switch, to add a small permanent stat ([[dragon-stones]]). Every use also raises the item's level requirement. You can repeat it until:

- the stat reaches its D-stoning cap, which counts only what D-stoning added (enhanced damage and defense can never pass 500% in total), or
- the item's level requirement penalty reaches the fail-safe limit for its tier (normal and jewelry allow the most, elite the least).

D-stoning cannot be removed. It works on runewords too: done to the white base before you make the runeword, it survives removing the runes; done afterwards, it is lost with the runes.

### D-stoning caps

Each cap counts only what D-stoning added: 190% fire spell damage from affixes still leaves room for the full +300%. A use applies while the D-stoned total is at or below the cap in the recipe, so the last use can go a few points past the official page's round number.

::dstone-caps

On armor, Blackmoor + Key also fills the separate strength, dexterity and energy caps. The official page differs (ESR 3.3) on the weapon Forging Hammer (no cap check in the recipe; page 500%), Blackmoor + Key on weapons (page 150), Holy Symbol on armor (page 150) and Forging Hammer + Stamina Potion on jewelry (page 400%). Its Void Shard + Stamina Potion elemental damage recipe takes a Forging Hammer in the game files, where the physical spell damage recipe comes first.

### Gem melding

:term[Gem melding] uses a Perfect Gem and Ancient Decipherers to add that colour's chipped-gem bonus for the item's slot. It also raises the level requirement and has a per-item limit, higher for weapons.

::recipes[gem-meld]

### Mapling

:term[Mapling] is Secret Recipe 50: the item, a :term[Maple Leaf] and a Perfect Gem (a Sa Rune before 3.2.13) lower the level requirement penalty a little, so the fail-safe opens again and you can D-stone more. The loop D-stone → Maple → D-stone is one of the strongest upgrades in ESR, and the most expensive.

::secret-recipe[50]

Maple Leaves come mainly from spare unique charms ([[unique-charms]]) and Secret Recipes 26 and 40 ([[secret-recipes]]). Orbs of Anointment stopped making Maple Leaves in 3.3 ([[corruption-anointment]]).

::recipes[maple-leaf]

If you D-stoned a white base before making a runeword, remove the runes before Mapling, or the materials are wasted.
