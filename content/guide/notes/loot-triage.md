Early on, pick up more than feels sensible. Most ESR materials become points in :term[Stocker]s, so they cost almost no space ([[stockers]]). Later you will stop picking up the cheap ones. Found something you don't recognise? Look it up on the [Materials page](page:/game-data/materials): where it comes from and which recipes use it.

### Always keep

- :term[Ancient Decipherer]s: they identify scrolls and feed many recipes; eight make a Dragon Stone.
- Dragon Stones in any of their forms ([[dragon-stones]]).
- :term[Ancient Scroll]s: each unlocks a Secret Recipe ([[secret-recipes]]).
- :term[Ancient Coupon]s and Wild Cards: they become the vanilla uniques ([[coupons]]).
- Runes, Decals, gems and crystals ([[rune-families]], [[gems-crystals]]).
- :term[Organ]s: tinkering, the Vessel of Souls and many conversions. Steaks, Cookbooks and Flags are not organs but are also recipe inputs.
- Socket Donuts, Thawing Potions, Keys and Worldstone Shards.
- Unique charms: never sell them ([[unique-charms]]).
- Mystical Shards (3.3, from strong monsters in endgame-level zones): ten make a Mystical Tesseract, which unlocks a specialization ([[specializations]]). Since 3.3.02 they go in the stash's currency tab.
- Ores: sell them for gold or smash them for crystals.

### Keep while you learn

- Jewels, magic and rare charms, rings and amulets: they cube into better versions or go into a Rerolling Orb.
- Unwanted set and unique gear: a Multi Stocker turns it into Decipherer points; elite and ethereal pieces give more.
- Rejuvenation potions: they cube up into Full Rejuvenation and appear in many recipes.
- Devil's Food: selling it to a vendor makes Diablo Clone spawn.

### Usually sell

- Ordinary white, magic and rare weapons and armor you will not use as a base. Check the socket count first if you plan a runeword ([[runewords-gemwords]]).
