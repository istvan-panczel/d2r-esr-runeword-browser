The :term[Noob's Odd Charm] from the [[starter-pack]] has one socket. Put whatever helps right now into it, such as a rune for run speed or a gem for resistances, and swap it later; a Key in the cube takes the socketable out again. Like every charm, it only works from the charm inventory, and only one of Noob's or Veteran's Odd Charm counts at a time.

### Upgrading it

Secret Recipe 49 turns it into a **Veteran's Odd Charm** with one socket:

::secret-recipe[49]

You need Ancient Scroll 49 (it comes back after the recipe), seven rare charms, an Elixir and a :term[Maple Leaf]. Secret Recipe 1 makes Elixirs from a Cookbook and a Steak ([[secret-recipes]]). Many players do this in Nightmare or Hell, once Maple Leaves start to come in.

### Sockets on the Veteran's charm

- A Socket Donut plus a Perfect Gem adds one socket, up to three. Socketables in it give their helm bonus.
- Runewords must be removed before adding a socket.
- A Thawing Potion resets the charm to one socket and returns one Perfect Gem per added socket; empty the sockets first.
- Forging recipes do not work on it.

The reset is listed with the other charm recipes, which also reroll the magic and rare charms you find (since 3.3.03 the result is the same charm type you put in):

::recipes[charm-reroll]

A runeword or gemword that allows charms as its base needs exactly as many sockets as it has runes or gems, so pick the socket count to match; the [Runewords](page:/runewords) page shows which ones ([[runewords-gemwords]]). See [[socketing]] for sockets on other items.
