An :term[Ascendancy] is a subclass granted by an Ascendancy Stone, a charm with its own skills and bonuses. To get one, kill the five special superuniques on Hell, cube their five elemental orbs into an Orb of Creation, then cube the orb with Blemished gems; the colours pick the ascendancy ([[gems-crystals]]).

### Where the five orbs drop

Each orb drops from one superunique on Hell. The official page only says which acts have one; the places below were checked in game. Since 3.3 the five orbs and the Orb of Creation cannot be put in the shared stash, so farm them with the character that will wear the stone.

**Orb of Storms:** Act 1, The Hole level 2, below the Black Marsh.

::source[Orb of Storms]

**Orb of Earth:** Act 2, Halls of the Dead level 2, below the Dry Hills.

::source[Orb of Earth]

**Orb of Water:** Act 3, Travincal, south-east of the Council.

::source[Orb of Water]

**Orb of Wind:** Act 5, Arreat Plateau, west of the Crystalline Passage entrance.

::source[Orb of Wind]

**Orb of Fire:** Act 5, Worldstone Chamber, north-west corner.

::source[Orb of Fire]

### Making and upgrading the stone

Each later tier is a challenge finished in the cube: Nihlathak on Hell, all three Uber Tristram bosses within a minute, [Primordial Soulstones](<dropfinder:Primordial Soulstone>) from map bosses (since 3.2.22 they stop dropping once your Ascendancy Stone holds ten), and a tier 5 map boss ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]]). Tiers can be done in any order.

::recipes[ascendancy]

You can wear one stone at a time. It cannot be moved to another character, corrupted or anointed. Older official pages said you must re-equip it after a death; the 3.2.24 page no longer does ([[death-and-respec]]). <!-- verify: the docs dropped the re-equip warning between 3.2.20 and 3.2.24 without a patch note; check in game whether the bonuses survive a death --> Compare them on the app's [Ascendancies](page:/ascendancies) page, which also lists the orb locations and the tier challenges.
