There is no fixed order after Hell Baal. A path many players follow:

1. **Hell special areas** such as the Secret Cow Level and Harpie's Nest, for experience and materials. Raise the players setting as you get stronger.
2. **Naraku and Nihlathak's Domain**, below Act 5's Ancients' Way. The official Maps page calls these the hardest special areas. Their superuniques and Nihlathak can drop tier 1 Endgame Maps, and killing Nihlathak on Hell is an Ascendancy challenge.
3. **The first Ascendancy:** five special superuniques on Hell drop the pieces of your Ascendancy Stone ([[ascendancies]]).
4. **Diablo Clone and Uber Tristram** for [Annihilus](dropfinder:Annihilus) and the Hellfire Torch ([[unique-charms]]); selling Devil's Food spawns Diablo Clone, and the Pandemonium keys lead to Uber Tristram ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]]).
5. **:term[Endgame map]s:** five tiers of mostly boss zones, the main loop of the endgame and the only source of mythical uniques ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]], [[endgame-bosses]]). The official page recommends roughly 5k life, [maximum resistances](page:/modifiers?kind=resist-fire) and some [physical resistance](page:/modifiers?kind=physical-resist) before you start.

Along the way:

- A :term[Terror zone] raises its monsters' level. Terrorized champions and uniques have a small chance at a [Worldstone Shard](<dropfinder:Worldstone Shard>) for [[corruption-anointment]], and terrorized superuniques and act bosses at the highest monster level (96) can also drop tier 1 maps; terrorized map bosses add several shards ([[endgame-bosses]]). Since 3.3 every zone belongs to a terror group, each group also terrorizes one Endgame Map, and terrorized campaign zones hold more monsters.
- **Heralds** in terror zones no longer wait for part of the zone to be cleared (3.3); they count as bosses and prime evils ([[hell-survival]]) and gain a rare affix every tier.
- Since 3.3 you gain less experience from level 70 on, and far less past level 100; each level still needs the same experience.
- **Corrupted Zakarum** appear at random in Nightmare and Hell and can drop [Weapon Mastery Tokens](<dropfinder:Weapon Mastery Token>) ([[weapon-mastery]]).
- After an endgame boss kill you have 60 seconds to cube your Kill Ledger with the listed materials for a permanent bonus ([[kill-ledger]]).
- The [Vessel of Souls](<dropfinder:Vessel of Souls>), a map drop, turns organs into bonuses and later a mythical weapon ([[vessel-of-souls]]).
- Dying inside a map boss area costs no experience ([[death-and-respec]]).

Browse what the maps can drop on the [Mythicals](page:/mythicals) page, and keep improving your gear with [[enhancement-order]].
