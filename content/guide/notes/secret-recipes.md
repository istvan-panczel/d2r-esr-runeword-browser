Each :term[Secret Recipe] has a number, and it only works with the identified :term[Ancient Scroll] of that number in the cube. The scroll comes back after the recipe, so one scroll serves for good. A Decipherer is spent only once, when you identify the scroll; older guides that say each recipe uses one are out of date.

### Getting the scroll you need

- Scrolls drop unidentified. Cube one with an :term[Ancient Decipherer] to reveal its number.

::recipes[ancient-scrolls]

Scrolls live in the stash's currency tab, and since 3.2.11 their description shows there. A scroll steps down one number with Alt + click in the stash or, since 3.3.03, when you cube it on its own (the block above), so never transmute a lone scroll by accident.

### The ones to look for first

- **1** makes Elixirs from a Cookbook and a Steak. This plain Elixir is a cube ingredient that feeds several of the recipes below, not one of the drinkable elixirs that drop since 3.3 ([[elixirs]]).
- **16 and 17** add sockets to white weapons and armor while keeping ethereal, superior and staff bonuses ([[socketing]]).
- **25** smashes an Ore for a chance at a crystal ([[gems-crystals]]).
- **26 and 40** make Maple Leaves from Dragon Stones and Perfect Gems, or from Devil's Food.
- **31 to 36** turn rare, set, crafted or unique belts, gloves and boots into another of those slots with the same bonuses.
- **45 to 48** turn small and grand charms into square and odd charms with a socket.
- **49** upgrades the starting charm ([[noobs-charm]]).
- **50** is Mapling, the key to repeated D-stoning ([[dstoning-and-mapling]]).

Numbers have shifted between ESR versions, so trust the list below, which is read from the game files.
