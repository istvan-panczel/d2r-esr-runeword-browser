Specializations arrived in ESR 3.3. Strong monsters in endgame-level zones drop **Mystical Shards**; cube ten of them into a **Mystical Tesseract** and use it to unlock specializations and gain a point. More points come on their own at levels 45, 60, 75 and 90. A character already past those levels gets the points for its level when it uses the Tesseract (three at level 63). You can have at most five.

::source[Mystical Shard]

There are four specializations:

- **Alchemist:** potions, including the drinkable elixirs ([[elixirs]]).
- **Blacksmith:** weapons and armor.
- **Jeweler:** rings, amulets, gems and jewels.
- **Archaeologist:** drops, coupons, runes and special artefacts.

Each has 15 perks in five rows of three; you pick one perk per row. Shift-click a perk to unlearn it, which takes five respecs from your counter ([[death-and-respec]]). To switch specialization, unlearn every perk of the old one first.

Some perks change cube recipes from other notes: [True Master](page:/specializations?tree=blacksmith&perk=true-master) puts a Weapon Mastery Token on any weapon type ([[weapon-mastery]]); [The Secret Crucible](page:/specializations?tree=blacksmith&perk=the-secret-crucible) and [Sanctimonious](page:/specializations?tree=blacksmith&perk=sanctimonious) allow a second Forging and a second anointment on weapons and armor; [Hallowed Accessories](page:/specializations?tree=jeweler&perk=hallowed-accessories) lets you sanctify rings and amulets ([[enhancement-order]]); [Power Overwhelming](page:/specializations?tree=archaeologist&perk=power-overwhelming) lets you make runewords in magic items and socket magic weapons and armor as if they were white ([[socketing]]). The Jeweler's gem perks raise socketed gems and jewels the way the [Increased Effect of Socketed Gems](page:/modifiers?kind=stat-gem-effect) stats do, and add up with them. The official page lists every perk; the [Specializations](page:/specializations) page shows each tree with every perk and what the game files say about it.

Two perks do not work as written in 3.3. The [Elixir of Reincarnation](page:/specializations?tree=alchemist&perk=elixir-of-reincarnation) has no cube recipe yet ([[elixirs]]), and [Alien Artefacts](page:/specializations?tree=archaeologist&perk=alien-artefacts) drop for every player, perk or not; the mod author says the next patch fixes both. [Deep Pockets](page:/specializations?tree=archaeologist&perk=deep-pockets) works even though nothing in the game files shows it: the mod author says the plugin grants it directly.
