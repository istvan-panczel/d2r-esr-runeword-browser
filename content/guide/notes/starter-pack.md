A new ESR character starts with a few unusual items. Put the **Starter Pack** into the Horadric Cube on its own and transmute. For your class the game files give:

::recipe-output[Adventurer's Pack]

- :term[Noob's Odd Charm]: a charm with one socket. Fill the socket with whatever helps now; later it becomes a Veteran's Odd Charm ([[noobs-charm]]).
- :term[Kill Ledger]: counts your kills and takes permanent bonuses. Keep it in your inventory at all times ([[kill-ledger]]).

The official cube page also lists a :term[Character Augmenter] in the pack. The 3.2.12 game files have no recipe, drop or starting item that gives it, so do not expect one.

Your starting inventory also holds:

- a **Weapons Crate**: cube it alone for a set of class starter weapons;
- an **Elixir of Skill**: drink it for one permanent skill point (the Elixir of Skill that drops since 3.3 is a different, 45-second utility elixir);
- an **Introductory Book**: a few pages of tips; cube it alone to turn the page;
- your class's chipped gem, which Skill Forging uses later.

Charms only work from the separate charm inventory, not the normal one ([[avoid-disasters]]). If you lose the pack, a Tome of Town Portal plus a Tome of Identify in the cube makes a new one.
