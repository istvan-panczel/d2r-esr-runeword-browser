The :term[Dragon Stone] is ESR's workhorse material. It drops, eight Ancient Decipherers cube into one, and since 3.3 a spare Orb of Anointment and a Key cube into two ([[corruption-anointment]]). Keep every one you find in a Multi Stocker ([[stockers]]).

### The forms

Cube Dragon Stones on their own and they turn into the next of their :term[Dragon Stone forms], round and round:

::recipes[dstone-cycle]

Each form is a different ingredient. In [[dstoning-and-mapling]] the form decides which stat is added; class crafts ([[class-crafting]]), Secret Recipes and [[weapon-mastery]] each ask for specific forms. Older guides list seven forms; the Void Shard joined later.

::source[Dragon Stone]

### Anvil Stones

An :term[Anvil Stone] is the costlier stone, made from Dragon Stones (or Multi Stocker points) and a Perfect Gem:

::recipes[anvil-stone]

Anvil Stones pay for Skill, All Skill and Stat Forging ([[forging]]), Merc Only Conversion ([[mercenaries-oskills]]) and [[weapon-mastery]]. Removing a Forging or the conversion gives them back; removing a weapon mastery does not.

::source[Anvil Stone]

### Spending them well

Dragon Stones and Anvil Stones run out fast once you start D-stoning and forging every slot. Early on, put them into gear you will keep: Forging can be removed, but D-stoning is permanent, so do not D-stone a placeholder item ([[enhancement-order]]).
