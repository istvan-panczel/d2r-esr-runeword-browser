Eastern Sun Resurrected (ESR) is a total conversion of Diablo II Resurrected by CelestialRayOne. If you know vanilla D2R, these are the five things that will surprise you:

- **Most items are new or reworked.** ESR has its own runes (:term[ES rune]s), :term[Gemword]s, coupon-made vanilla uniques and mythical uniques. Vanilla runewords still exist, but their runes are built from parts ([[rune-families]]).
- **The Horadric Cube is your main tool.** Hundreds of recipes reroll, upgrade and enhance gear. Read [[cube-basics]] before you spend rare materials.
- **Materials pile up fast.** Organs, Dragon Stones, coupons and scrolls drop all the time; a :term[Stocker] keeps most of them as points and scrolls sit in the stash's currency tab ([[loot-triage]]).
- **The campaign is harder.** Monsters and skills are rebalanced, and the step from Nightmare into Hell is steep ([[hell-survival]]). Character level goes up to 110.
- **The endgame is new.** Endgame Maps, Ascendancies, Diablo Clone and Uber Tristram are reworked ([[endgame-roadmap]]).

### How to use this guide

The five numbered steps on the [Guide](page:/guide) page follow a first character from town to the endgame. Read them in order the first time; every note is short, lists what to know first, and points to the next one. Before you leave the Rogue Encampment, read [[starter-pack]] and [[avoid-disasters]].

When you pick up something strange, start with [[loot-triage]]. Unknown words are underlined and collected in the [[glossary]]. Since 3.3 the game also has Sully's wiki built in: open it from the cube button or the main menu, or set a hotkey.

Lists and numbers in these notes come from the game files, so they update with each ESR release. The official site stays the full reference; each note links to the matching page.
