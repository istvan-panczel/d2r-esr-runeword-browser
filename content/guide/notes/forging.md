:term[Forging] adds one extra bonus to an item without any penalty. An item can hold only one Forging at a time, but you can remove it and forge again.

### Kinds of Forging

- **Skill Forging:** +1 to one class's skills; the chipped gem picks the class (Obsidian is the Warlock).
- **All Skill Forging:** +1 to all skills, paid with a Maple Leaf, an Anvil Stone and any chipped gem.
- **Stat Forging:** one stat, such as life, [faster cast rate](page:/modifiers?kind=fcr) or resistances, depending on the gem and the item slot.
- **Rune Forging:** two identical ES or Kanji runes and any chipped gem add that rune's bonus; LoD runes cannot be forged. It is cheap and useful from Normal on.
- **Aura Forging:** amulets only; the Aura Stone picks the aura.

Targets are equipment of any rarity, white and socketed items included, but not runewords. Unique jewels and charms take Skill and Stat Forging; magic and rare jewels have a smaller Stat Forging and a Rune Forging for low runes, where a Scroll of Town Portal or Identify picks the weapon or helm bonus.

### Removing it

Cube the item with three :term[Thawing Potion]s. The Anvil Stones, runes or Maple Leaf come back (the chipped gem does not); an Aura Stone is destroyed, unless you cube the amulet with two Perfect gem 6 Packs and a Maple Leaf instead, which hands the Aura Stone back (3.2.20 game files). A Wo Forging cannot be removed at all, because its "Indestructible" bonus cannot be taken away.

::recipes[remove-forging]

### Things to know

- Most reroll recipes ignore forged items, so remove the Forging before rerolling ([[cube-basics]]).
- Merc Only Conversion stacks with a Forging ([[mercenaries-oskills]]).
- Secret Recipes 8 and 9 add bonus experience or gold to a weapon as a Forging ([[secret-recipes]]).

Forging is the safest enhancement because it can be undone; see [[enhancement-order]] for where it fits.
