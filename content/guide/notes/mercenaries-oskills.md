ESR's mercenaries have new skill sets that change with their type and the difficulty you hire them in; a Hell Combat Desert Mercenary, for example, brings Conviction. :term[Oskill]s are skills that items grant to any class, such as the elemental Surges and Brands. The official page lists both.

:term[Merc Only Conversion] cubes a weapon or armor (not a runeword) with an Anvil Stone and a Perfect Gem. The gem picks a bonus package, such as +1 to all skills with [Deadly Strike](page:/modifiers?kind=deadly-strike), that only a mercenary can use: the item shows "(merc only)" and gets a hidden life drain that empties your own life to 1 ([[avoid-disasters]]). Three Antidote Potions undo it and return the Anvil Stone and the gem ([[dragon-stones]]). It stacks with a Forging ([[enhancement-order]]).

::recipes[merc-only]
