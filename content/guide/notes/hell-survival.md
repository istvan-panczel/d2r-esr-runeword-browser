ESR's campaign is harder than vanilla, and the jump from late Nightmare into Hell is the steepest part. The difficulty penalties, from the game files:

::difficulty-penalties

Normal's small positive resistance value offsets hidden Kill Ledger effects and cancels out while the Kill Ledger is equipped; the mod author plans to set it to 0 in the next patch.

In short: your resistances drop sharply, monsters get extra skill levels, and your freezes, chills and some curses on them wear off sooner. :term[Leech] is divided by the numbers above, so most item leech is close to nothing in Hell, and only physical weapon damage leeches; [Life on Striking](page:/modifiers?kind=on-striking) and Life after each Kill are not reduced. You and your summons deal less damage to :term[Prime evil]{label="prime evils"} in Nightmare and Hell, and your mercenary deals less to bosses in every difficulty. Since 3.3 monster spells hit about a fifth harder, and rare monster affixes keep growing past monster level 100.

### Before you enter Hell

- **Resistances:** get fire, cold, lightning and poison back to the cap after the :term[Resistance penalty]{label=penalty}, and add some [physical](page:/modifiers?kind=physical-resist) and magic resistance if you can.
- **Life:** Stat Forging with a chipped Topaz adds % maximum life ([[forging]]); the first Kill Ledger milestones add resistances and more ([[kill-ledger]]).
- **Damage:** Skill Forging (+1 to your class skills) on every slot is the standard early boost for skill-based builds. Plan a way past :term[Immunity]{label=immunities}, such as Lower Resist charges, a Conviction aura forged onto your amulet with a White Aura Stone, or a Hell Combat Desert Mercenary, whose skills include Conviction ([[mercenaries-oskills]]).
- **Materials:** farm Nightmare's special areas first. The official Maps page lists them (the Secret Cow Level, Blood Raven's Workshop, Lost Farm and others) and recommends them once you have beaten that difficulty.
- **Players setting:** raise it for more experience and drops when you are comfortable, and lower it when you are not.

### Know the layout

ESR changes several maps and moves some quest areas; a few vanilla zones are now dangerous special areas. Check the official Maps page when a quest area is not where you expect. Since 3.2.19 an :term[Evil Urn] can stand in any area with monsters (2%, 4% and 6% of rooms in Normal, Nightmare and Hell); breaking one releases a boss pack whose boss carries one extra rare affix.

Once Hell Baal is down, continue with [[endgame-roadmap]].
