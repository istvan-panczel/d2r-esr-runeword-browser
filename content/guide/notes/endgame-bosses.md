Each of the five map tiers has four maps, and 14 of the 20 have a boss; the other six are horde maps whose loot comes from their many unique packs. The [Campaign Map](page:/campaign-map?tab=maps) shows every zone with its boss, rewards and fight notes. The fight descriptions below come from the official Endgame Maps page, in our words; the drop chances come from the game files, so they can differ from the page.

### What they drop

Every boss rolls an Orb of Anointment ([[corruption-anointment]]), a map of the next tier, a Pandemonium key ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]]), a Weapon Mastery Token ([[weapon-mastery]]) and a Primordial Soulstone for [[ascendancies]], plus many rolls of ordinary loot. Raising the players setting to 8 roughly doubles the orb, map and key chances (in brackets); the token and the named drops stay the same. A terrorized tier 1 to 4 boss adds Worldstone Shards, more at higher tiers.

Where the official page lists other drop chances, the tables above follow the files: the page promises a key every time from tier 4 bosses and the Guardians of Time, and a whole key set from the other tier 5 bosses (the files drop one random key). Shards are different: the files give terrorized tier 5 bosses shards too. The official page used to say they drop none because of an engine limit, which the files cannot show, and in 3.3.03 it dropped that paragraph, so check in game before counting on them. The Eye of the Storm bosses drop Diablo's Demonic Horn, Mephisto's Demonic Brain and Baal's Demonic Eye, used to anoint unique charms. The Alien Artefact in every table is new in 3.3; since ESR 3.3.02 only Archaeologists with the [Alien Artefacts](page:/specializations?tree=archaeologist&perk=alien-artefacts) perk get it, and they go in the stash's currency tab (3.3.03; depositing them works since 3.3.04).

### Tiers 1 to 4

- **Lucion's Whisper** ([Cellar of Pain](page:/campaign-map?area=149)): long-burning fire on the ground and a petrifying curse that lowers fire resistance; he teleports away when hit.
- **Diablo's Hellhound** ([Chasm of Horrors](page:/campaign-map?area=141)): speeds up with every attack, which can also fire a meteor, a frozen orb, a bone spirit or a resistance curse.
- **Ancient Fallen Angel** ([Necropolis](page:/campaign-map?area=146)): a slow physical and magic nova you dodge by putting a wall between you, and lightning-ball storms.
- **Chaos Rift** ([map](page:/campaign-map?area=159)): two of the three bosses above at once, each with its own rewards.
- **Terror in the Shadows** ([Infested Lair](page:/campaign-map?area=154)): leaps, poisonous spiderlings and a slowing web ball that lowers poison resistance.
- **Avatar of the Night** ([Labyrinth of Suffering](page:/campaign-map?area=162)): a pounce that strips physical and cold resistance, bouncing javelins, sticky ice bolts and crushing blow.
- **Herald of Doom** ([Endless Abyss](page:/campaign-map?area=175), the tenth level): meteors, a homing lightning ring and sticky ice; he pierces elemental resistance, so overcap it.
- **Glacial Behemoth** and **The Taskmaster** ([Frozen Wastes](page:/campaign-map?area=179)), one per descent, both without teleport: an ice avalanche and a petrifying curse; immune slaves that he enrages or turns into exploding runners.
- **Baal's Disfigured Harbinger of Destruction** ([Shrine of Destruction](page:/campaign-map?area=184)): homing cold novas, lightning storms and healing beasts every 30 seconds (kill them away from him); he pierces elemental resistance.
- **Arbiter of Souls** ([Reliquary of Souls](page:/campaign-map?area=190)): bone spirits and bone spears, a rare soul storm, and a curse that slows you and lowers magic resistance.

### Tier 5: hurting the invincible

Tier 5 bosses cannot be damaged until you pick up an empowerment, often two at once:

- **Eye of the Storm** ([map](page:/campaign-map?area=193)) holds three bosses, all piercing elemental resistance. **Mephisto**: grab the light orbs that sometimes appear when he is hit. **Diablo**: hitting him calls angels; when he kills them they empower everyone near them. **Baal**: destroy the towers he raises.
- **Guardians of Time** ([Icecrown Citadel](page:/campaign-map?area=203)): Talic, Madawc and Korlic need both Heaven's Light (light orbs from hitting Korlic) and the Eternal Pendulum's buff (destroy a pendulum Talic summons). Kill all three in one game: a launcher plugin rule drops the Eternal Hourglass of the Timeless Sands from the last one ([[unique-charms]]).
- **The Lich King** ([Icecrown Citadel](page:/campaign-map?area=202)): needs Light's Hope (kill the valkyries his pounce brings) and Heaven's Light (from his abominations). Watch for Eternal Winter, which cuts cold and physical resistance.
- **Rathma** ([Sanctum of the Dead](page:/campaign-map?area=206), no teleport): needs the effigies' buff (kill them before they crumble) and a Burning Soul from the dead he raises after his bone maze; leave the maze before it detonates. Kor'nac fights beside him, grows stronger over time and drops nothing.
- **Lucion** ([Reliquary of False Light](page:/campaign-map?area=211)): needs the buff his high priests leave when they die and a few seconds in his cleansing flames.

### Players 8 (uber)

On players 8 the last three tier 5 fights get harder (more and stronger adds; Rathma enrages at a quarter of his life; Lucion's aura grows without limit) and give something extra:

- **Lich King**: the kill adds +10% to your ascendancy's tier 3 and 4 scaling bonuses, by itself since 3.3.04; it used to give +1 to all skills ([[ascendancies]]).
- **Rathma**: kill him and Kor'nac within a minute of each other, then cube Rathma's Eternal Skull by itself in that minute for magic resist, maximum poison resist and Raise Kor'nac.
- **Lucion**: he also drops Lucion's Essence, a stronger socketing consumable.

::source[Lucion's Essence]

After any of these kills you also have 60 seconds for the [[kill-ledger]]; since 3.3.04 every player in the boss's area gets the kill. Dying in a map boss area costs no experience ([[death-and-respec]]).
