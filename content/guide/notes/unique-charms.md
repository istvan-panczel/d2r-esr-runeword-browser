Unique charms only work from the charm inventory, and ESR limits how many count at once ([[avoid-disasters]]).

### The boss charms

**Annihilus** [drops from Diablo Clone](dropfinder:Annihilus), who spawns when you sell Devil's Food to a vendor.

::card[unique:Annihilus]

**Hellfire Torch** drops when all three Uber Tristram bosses die in the same game ([[maps-keys-ascendancy]] covers the keys).

::source[Hellfire Torch]

The **Eternal Hourglass of the Timeless Sands** drops finished from the Guardians of Time in the Icecrown Citadel, a tier 5 Endgame Map: kill Talic, Madawc and Korlic in one game and a launcher plugin rule drops it from the last one ([[endgame-bosses]]). Its old pieces and their cube recipe are still in the files, but nothing drops them. [Rathma's Eternal Skull](<dropfinder:Rathma's Eternal Skull>) comes from Rathma's tier 5 map ([[endgame-roadmap]]).

::source[Eternal Hourglass of the Timeless Sands]

### Making them stronger

- A **Standard of Heroes**, [dropped by the uber bosses](<dropfinder:Standard of Heroes>), upgrades one unique charm once with a small bonus: [every charm's bonus](page:/enhance?tab=standard).
- [Diablo's Demonic Horn](<dropfinder:Diablo's Demonic Horn>), Baal's Demonic Eye and Mephisto's Demonic Brain together anoint a unique charm once ([[corruption-anointment]]).
- Most unique charms reroll with Perfect Gems; the cost depends on the charm's required level.
- Skill and Stat :term[Forging] accept unique charms ([[forging]]).

::recipes[unique-charm-upgrades]

### Spare ones

A spare unique charm, any Rejuvenation Potion and a Key make a :term[Maple Leaf], the fuel for Mapling and All Skill Forging ([[dstoning-and-mapling]]). Remove any Forging first. A Multi Stocker can do the same and keep the leaf as a point.
